Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions
Key Summary: Rexo Calculator is an offline-first calculator tool. We do not collect, sell, or track any personal information or financial data. Calculations remain strictly on your local device. Non-intrusive advertisements are served via Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy applies to the Rexo Calculator mobile application developed and published by Rexo Agency. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through full transparency and compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Rexo Calculator does not require user registration, account creation, or login credentials. The application does not collect, transmit, store, or share:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Banking, credit/debit card, or transactional payment details.
  • Precise or coarse physical location data.
  • Contacts, microphone recordings, camera feeds, or photo storage.
  • Mathematical values, prices, tax amounts, or calculations entered by the user.

All calculations (including GST/Tax calculations, weight/price conversions, volume rates, discounts, and percentage operations) are computed in real time entirely within your device's memory.

3. Local Device Data Storage

Any calculation history or currency preference is saved exclusively in local sandboxed device memory. This data is never transmitted to any external server or cloud service. You can clear your calculation history at any time with a single tap in the app.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

To keep the application free for everyone, Rexo Calculator displays non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device telemetry (such as device model, OS version, coarse diagnostic signals, and mobile advertising identifiers) to display relevant ads in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies and applicable privacy regulations.

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Rexo Calculator is designed as a general utility tool and does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our advertising configurations adhere to Google Play's Families policy guidelines.

6. Security

Because the app does not maintain any backend database or transmit personal user data across the internet, the risk of data compromise or external data breaches is virtually zero.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain alignment with evolving Google Play policies or regulatory standards. The updated policy will always be published at this permanent URL.
